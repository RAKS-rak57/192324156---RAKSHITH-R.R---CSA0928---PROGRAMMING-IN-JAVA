=== 6. Life cycle: ticket becomes garbage after exit ===

=== 7. Space used by the counter ===
static  counter: 1 int for the whole class, O(1) for k facilities
instance counter: 1 int per facility object, O(k)
tickets (n): O(n) in both designs
