=== 3. Starvation and the aging (fair) rule ===
Scenario: a severity-5 patient arrives at time 0. At every time unit one new patient (severity 1) arrives and
one patient is served. The queue starts with 10 waiting severity-1 patients, so it never becomes empty.
(worst case: every new arrival is severity 1, i.e. only critical patients keep arriving)
Plain PriorityQueue : severity-5 patient served at time NEVER (still waiting after 2000 time units)
Fair rule (aging, limit 50) : severity-5 patient served at time 200   (expected: 4 x 50 = 200 units, when his effective severity has dropped from 5 to 1 and his earlier arrival wins)

=== 4. Deployment: a city hospital with 5,000 patients a day (worst case: all 5,000 waiting together) ===
Plain PriorityQueue : admit + serve 5,000 patients = 7.48 ms in total (1.496 microseconds per patient)
Fair (rebuild) rule : admit + serve 5,000 patients = 274.79 ms in total (54.958 microseconds per patient)
