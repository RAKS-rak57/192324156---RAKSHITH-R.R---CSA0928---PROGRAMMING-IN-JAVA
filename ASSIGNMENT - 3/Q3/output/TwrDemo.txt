$ java -cp out TwrDemo
=== 1. Closing order: reverse of opening ===
  open  A (opened first)
  open  B (opened second)
  body runs
  close B (opened second)
  close A (opened first)

=== 2. Body fails AND close() fails -> the close failure is SUPPRESSED, the body failure is reported ===
  open  A
  open  B
  close B
  close A
  caught   : body failed (the PRIMARY exception)
  suppressed (2): [java.lang.IllegalStateException: close() of B failed, java.lang.IllegalStateException: close() of A failed]

=== 3. The same situation with manual finally -> the ORIGINAL failure is lost ===
  open  A
  open  B
  close B
  close A
  caught   : close() of A failed   <- the body failure and B's close failure are GONE
  suppressed (0): nothing recorded
