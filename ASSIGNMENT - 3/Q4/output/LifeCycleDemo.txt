$ java -cp out LifeCycleDemo
after new Thread(...)          : NEW   isAlive=false
50 ms after start(), computing : RUNNABLE   isAlive=true
while inside Thread.sleep(300) : TIMED_WAITING
after the run() method ended   : TERMINATED   isAlive=false
waiting to enter synchronized  : BLOCKED
inside wait()                  : WAITING
inside join() of another thread: WAITING

A scanner station (Thread / Runnable) passes through: NEW -> RUNNABLE -> TIMED_WAITING (each sleep) <-> RUNNABLE -> TERMINATED.
main passes through WAITING while it is inside join().
